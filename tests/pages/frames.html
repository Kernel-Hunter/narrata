Harbour Diary

The fishing boats came in early today. By seven the quay was busy with crates, gulls and people in heavy boots, and the smell of diesel hung over the water until the wind picked up.

Below is the weather report from the harbour office, which they publish on their own page. The diary continues after it.

In the afternoon the wind turned north and the sea went grey. The last boat came in at four, and the harbour master closed the gate behind it with a long rusty squeal that everyone on the quay pretended not to hear.

Tomorrow the market opens at six. The diary will be back in the evening.
